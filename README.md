# 🎓 Sarthi of Student

### Your Guide from Campus to Career

Sarthi of Student is a career and placement-readiness platform designed to help students understand their current career position, identify skill and profile gaps, and take practical steps toward becoming placement-ready.

The platform focuses on helping students **Learn, Build, Grow, and Succeed** by bringing career preparation tools into one place.

---

## 🚀 Why Sarthi of Student?

Many students prepare for placements without knowing:

- Which skills they are missing
- Whether their resume is strong enough
- How their LinkedIn profile looks to recruiters
- Whether their GitHub demonstrates their technical abilities
- Which projects they should build
- What they should focus on for their target role

Sarthi of Student aims to make this process more structured and easier to understand.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📄 Resume Analyzer
Analyze your resume and identify areas that can be improved based on your target career or role.

### 💼 LinkedIn Profile Optimizer
Review important parts of your LinkedIn profile and provide suggestions to improve your professional presence.

### 💻 GitHub Analyzer
Analyze your GitHub profile and repositories to identify opportunities to improve your technical portfolio.

### 🎯 Career & Skill Gap Analysis
Understand the difference between your current profile and the skills commonly required for your target role.

### 🚀 Project Recommendations
Get project ideas based on your career goals and areas where your portfolio can be strengthened.

### 📝 Resume Builder
Create and improve your resume using structured career information and role-focused recommendations.

### 📊 Placement Readiness
Bring your resume, skills, projects, LinkedIn, and GitHub into one overall view to understand where you currently stand.
